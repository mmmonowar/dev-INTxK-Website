Hi! I'm Mustafa, I'm the creator of The Polymod Framework.

Believe it or not, this framework has been in development for more than 5 years now. Over the years, the framework has expanded into its own methodology and ecosystem.

I'll leave the complicated parts elsewhere for now. But if you haven't done it already, I highly recommend going through the [homepage](https://intxk.github.io/polymodframework). It should have a good overview, so I always recommend starting from there. It's a great point to look out and get a glimpse of what this is all about.

My goal is to offer you a powerful system that aids you every day, as a single source of truth, so that you can use it with the tools you already know and love.

My work with The Polymod Framework and The PolyOS has emerged out of my own necessities.

I will try to introduce PolyOS in detail in another post, In Sha Allah. For now, understand that what I'm building at this moment is a recipe, a blueprint that is very useful if you are interested in the 'write to think' methodology. The Polymod Framework and The PolyOS exist to aid you in your thinking and sensemaking.

Again, the idea here is to bring you proven productivity techniques and tools that are adaptable to changing technological circumstances. 

Personally, I believe in practising before preaching. If the updates feel slow, that is because I want to test my ideas first and validate if they are worth adopting. Only then do I officially teach them.

If you have read the description, you'll see that I am currently focusing on SEYEs. SEYE stands for Solo, Early-Stage and Young Entrepreneurs. These are people from the younger generation, or tired job-holders who wish who wishes to be financially independent and well off. They have ideas, and they want to be successful.

As a solo entrepreneur myself, I think I have some minimal understanding of how quickly the smiles and the aspirations are marred by crisis after crisis.

But at the end of the day, it's a journey. Success takes more than hard work. It is a topic entirely of its own. 

Both The Polymod Framework and the PolyOS are complementary. The PolyOS gives you the tool, but without being aware of the Framework, I worry that users might not extract the minimal value out of it.

For now, think of these two as a philosophy and a software application.

  > The Polymod Framework is meant to help you integrate your thoughts, actions, learning and growth into a coherent story which you can consult at any time to think. And by thinking, I mean going really deep into a subject matter.

  > As for the software, it is an opinionated tool for storing information digitally and using it as a decision support system.

There are so many things I want to share with you. We are just getting started with this blog. In sha Allah, I wish to bring you insights, knowledge and tools from my journey. 

So stay with me, because I want to do the same for you.
